Technical Exercise



Using Burlington, Ontario, Canada as the target market, please complete the following assignment:

Identify how many cannabis retail stores currently operate within Burlington and the surrounding area, using a 35 km radius.
Create a store list that includes:
Store name
Address
Phone number
Hours of operation
Owners Details
Collect the products each store is currently carrying, both on its website and in-store, along with listed pricing, including:
Regular price
Sale or promotional price
Duration of the sale or promotion, where applicable
Compare this data against platforms such as HiBuddy.ca and other relevant market sources.
Develop a business analytics summary that addresses the following:
Which products are carried across all or most stores?
What appears to be the sales velocity or sell-through potential of those products?
How often do these products change in price?
Which new and upcoming items are appearing through the Ontario Cannabis Store and similar channels?
What public commentary or sentiment is available about these products from sources such as Reddit?
Organize your findings into clear, presentable datasets and actionable insights that would help a company like MontKailash Cannabis outperform competitors in the market.
This task is intended to demonstrate your technical capability in data collection, analysis, competitive intelligence, and the presentation of business-useful outputs.
